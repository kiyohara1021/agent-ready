# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in `agentworthy` to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this project open, welcoming, diverse, and
healthy.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps this community:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- showing empathy toward other people

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
and commit history — and when an individual is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the maintainer
at **kiyohara@plan-k.co.jp**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the maintainer will respect the privacy and security of
the reporter.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this code, giving reasons when
appropriate. Consequences scale with the behavior, from a private warning
through to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
